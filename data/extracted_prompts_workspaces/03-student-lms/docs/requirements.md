# Requirements — Student Learning Management System

## Objective
Courses, enrollments, assignments, progress tracking.

## Functional requirements
- Courses.
- Enrollments.
- Assignments.
- Progress tracking.

## Non-functional requirements
- Validate inputs server-side.
- Provide health and OpenAPI endpoints.
- Keep secrets out of source control.
- Add persistent storage, access controls, and observability before production.
