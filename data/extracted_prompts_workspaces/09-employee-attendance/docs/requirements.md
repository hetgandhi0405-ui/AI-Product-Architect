# Requirements — Employee Attendance Management System

## Objective
Clock-in/out, attendance history, leave requests.

## Functional requirements
- Clock-in/out.
- Attendance history.
- Leave requests.

## Non-functional requirements
- Validate inputs server-side.
- Provide health and OpenAPI endpoints.
- Keep secrets out of source control.
- Add persistent storage, access controls, and observability before production.
