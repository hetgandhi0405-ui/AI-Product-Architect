# Requirements — Task Management Platform

## Objective
Projects, tasks, priorities, due dates, status, summary metrics.

## Functional requirements
- Projects.
- Tasks.
- Priorities.
- Due dates.
- Status.
- Summary metrics.

## Non-functional requirements
- Validate inputs server-side.
- Provide health and OpenAPI endpoints.
- Keep secrets out of source control.
- Add persistent storage, access controls, and observability before production.
