# Requirements — Personal Finance Tracker

## Objective
Income, expenses, budgets, owner-scoped summaries.

## Functional requirements
- Income.
- Expenses.
- Budgets.
- Owner-scoped summaries.

## Non-functional requirements
- Validate inputs server-side.
- Provide health and OpenAPI endpoints.
- Keep secrets out of source control.
- Add persistent storage, access controls, and observability before production.
