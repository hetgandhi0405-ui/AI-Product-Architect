# Requirements — Smart Home Monitoring Dashboard

## Objective
Devices, states, sensor readings; simulated readings are labelled.

## Functional requirements
- Devices.
- States.
- Sensor readings; simulated readings are labelled.

## Non-functional requirements
- Validate inputs server-side.
- Provide health and OpenAPI endpoints.
- Keep secrets out of source control.
- Add persistent storage, access controls, and observability before production.
