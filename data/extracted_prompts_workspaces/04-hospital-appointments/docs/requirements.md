# Requirements — Hospital Appointment Management System

## Objective
Providers, appointment booking, cancellation, schedule conflict checks; synthetic data only.

## Functional requirements
- Providers.
- Appointment booking.
- Cancellation.
- Schedule conflict checks; synthetic data only.

## Non-functional requirements
- Validate inputs server-side.
- Provide health and OpenAPI endpoints.
- Keep secrets out of source control.
- Add persistent storage, access controls, and observability before production.
