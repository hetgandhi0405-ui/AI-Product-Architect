# Requirements — E-Commerce Platform

## Objective
Product catalog, stock, development-mode orders, order history; never collect card details.

## Functional requirements
- Product catalog.
- Stock.
- Development-mode orders.
- Order history; never collect card details.

## Non-functional requirements
- Validate inputs server-side.
- Provide health and OpenAPI endpoints.
- Keep secrets out of source control.
- Add persistent storage, access controls, and observability before production.
