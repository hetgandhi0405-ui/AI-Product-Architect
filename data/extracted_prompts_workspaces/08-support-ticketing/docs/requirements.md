# Requirements — Customer Support Ticketing System

## Objective
Tickets, priorities, assignments, status transitions, comments.

## Functional requirements
- Tickets.
- Priorities.
- Assignments.
- Status transitions.
- Comments.

## Non-functional requirements
- Validate inputs server-side.
- Provide health and OpenAPI endpoints.
- Keep secrets out of source control.
- Add persistent storage, access controls, and observability before production.
